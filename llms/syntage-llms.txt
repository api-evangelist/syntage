# Syntage

## Docs

- [Accounts Payable Insight](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/accounts-payable-insight/get-accounts-payable.md): Returns accounts payable data showing amounts due and paid over time, including cumulative totals.
- [Accounts Receivable Insight](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/accounts-receivable-insight/get-accounts-receivable.md): Returns accounts receivable data showing amounts due and paid over time, including cumulative totals.
- [Retrieve a background check](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/background-checks/get-a-specific-background-check.md): Retrieves one background check, including its score, processing status, country scope, associated files, and links to related records.
- [List background checks](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/background-checks/get-all-background-checks.md): Lists background checks available to the organization. Use filters to narrow the list by processing status, country scope, or score.
- [List an entity's background checks](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/background-checks/get-an-entitys-background-checks.md): Lists background checks associated with an entity. Use this endpoint when your integration is working from a known entity ID.
- [Download background check PDF](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/background-checks/get-background-check-pdf-report.md): Downloads the generated PDF report for a background check. The response body is the PDF file itself.
- [List background check records](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/background-checks/get-background-check-records.md): Lists the records found during a background check. Records contain the detailed source data behind the check and are organized by category and database.
- [Balance Sheet Insight](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/balance-sheet-insight/get-balance-sheet-metric.md): Returns the entity's balance sheet organized in a tree of categories.
- [Buró de Crédito Summary Export](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/buro-de-credito-summary-insight/export-buro-de-credito-summary.md): Export Buró de Crédito report score summaries for an entity as CSV or XLSX.
- [Buró de Crédito Summary Insight](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/buro-de-credito-summary-insight/get-buro-de-credito-summary.md): List Buró de Crédito report score summaries for an entity.
- [Cash Flow Insight](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/cash-flow-insight/get-cash-flow.md): Inflows and outflows over time, derived from issued and received invoices.
- [Get a report's found entities](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/company-verification-reports/get-a-reports-found-entities.md): List every person or company identified in the source documents of a Mexican Company Verification report, with their cap-table holdings and powers of attorney expanded inline.
- [Retrieve a company verification report](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/company-verification-reports/get-a-specific-company-verification-report.md): Retrieves one company verification report, including its verification details, metadata, and supporting file references.
- [Get a specific found entity](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/company-verification-reports/get-a-specific-found-entity.md): Retrieve a single found entity, with its cap-table holdings and powers expanded inline.
- [List company verification reports](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/company-verification-reports/get-all-company-verification-reports.md): Lists company verification reports available to the organization. Use this endpoint when you need to page through reports across entities.
- [List an entity's company verification reports](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/company-verification-reports/get-an-entitys-company-verification-reports.md): Lists company verification reports associated with an entity. Use this endpoint when your integration is working from a known entity ID.
- [Current Powers Insight](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/corporate-structure-current-powers-insight/get-current-powers.md): List the people and companies that hold at least one currently effective power of attorney, drawn from the most recent Company Verification report registered for the entity.
- [Customer Concentration Insight](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/customer-concentration-insight/get-customer-invoice-concentration.md): Returns the concentration of issued or received invoices by customers.
- [Customer Network Insight](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/customer-network-insight/get-customer-network-insight.md): Customers the entity has sold to, with invoiced totals and cancellation rates per counterparty.
- [List an entity's BIL reports](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-bil-reports/list-an-entitys-bil-reports.md): List Buró de Investigaciones Legales reports generated for an entity.
- [Retrieve a BIL report](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-bil-reports/retrieve-a-bil-report.md): Retrieve a single Buró de Investigaciones Legales report by ID.
- [Create a Buró de Crédito authorization](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-buro-de-credito-authorizations/create-a-buro-de-credito-authorization.md): Create a Buró de Crédito authorization for an entity so report extractions can use it
- [Delete a Buró de Crédito authorization](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-buro-de-credito-authorizations/delete-a-buro-de-credito-authorization.md): Delete a Buró de Crédito authorization by authorization ID
- [Get a Buró de Crédito authorization](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-buro-de-credito-authorizations/get-a-buro-de-credito-authorization.md): Get a single Buró de Crédito authorization by authorization ID
- [Get Buró de Crédito authorizations](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-buro-de-credito-authorizations/get-all-buro-de-credito-authorizations.md): List Buró de Crédito authorizations for the organization
- [Get an entity's Buró de Crédito authorizations](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-buro-de-credito-authorizations/get-an-entitys-buro-de-credito-authorizations.md): List Buró de Crédito authorizations associated with an entity
- [Get a Buró de Crédito report](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-buro-de-credito-reports/get-a-buro-de-credito-report.md): Get a single Buró de Crédito report by report ID
- [Get an entity's Buró de Crédito reports](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-buro-de-credito-reports/get-an-entitys-buro-de-credito-reports.md): List Buró de Crédito reports generated for an entity
- [List an entity's RPC actos](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rpc-actos/list-an-entitys-rpc-actos.md): List RPC actos extracted for an entity. Use this endpoint when your integration needs individual registry acts rather than the complete RPC entidade response.
- [Retrieve RPC acto](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rpc-actos/retrieve-an-rpc-acto.md): Retrieve one RPC acto by ID, including registry act metadata, parsed act data, and file reference when a registry document is available.
- [List an entity's RPC entidades](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rpc-entidades/list-all-entities-rpc-entidades-data-beta.md): List RPC entidades extracted for an entity. Use this endpoint after an `rpc` extraction finishes to review the RPC company records found for the entity.
- [Retrieve RPC entidade](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rpc-entidades/retrieve-rpc-entidades-data-beta.md): Retrieve one RPC entidade by ID, including the company profile, related actos, and related socios extracted from Registro Publico de Comercio.
- [Retrieve RPC socio](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rpc-socios/retrieve-an-rpc-socio.md): Retrieve one RPC socio by ID, including shareholder details and the RPC entidade profile it belongs to.
- [List an entity's RUG guarantees](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rug-garantias/list-all-entities-rug-garantias-data-beta.md): List RUG guarantees extracted for an entity. Use this endpoint after a `rug` extraction finishes to review movable collateral guarantees found for the entity.
- [Retrieve RUG guarantee](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rug-garantias/retrieve-rug-garantia-data.md): Retrieve one RUG guarantee by ID, including collateral details, creditor, grantors, contract dates, and related operations.
- [List an entity's RUG operations](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rug-operaciones/list-all-entities-rug-operaciones-data.md): List RUG operations extracted for an entity. Use this endpoint after a `rug` extraction finishes to review registry acts related to the entity's guarantees.
- [Retrieve RUG operation](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-rug-operaciones/retrieve-rug-operacion-data.md): Retrieve one RUG operation by ID, including operation type, seat number, guarantee number, grantors, parsed boleta data, and file reference when available.
- [List an entity's SAT certificates](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-sat-certificates/list-an-entitys-sat-certificates.md): List SAT certificates associated with an entity. These digital certificates are used for electronic signatures and digital sealing in Mexico's tax system.
- [Retrieve a SAT certificate](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-sat-certificates/retrieve-a-sat-certificate.md): Retrieve a SAT certificate by ID. The `file` property references the certificate file. Use the file download endpoint to retrieve the certificate content.
- [Create a credential](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-sat-credentials/create-a-credential.md): Create a SAT credential for an RFC. If the RFC is not already associated with your organization, Syntage creates the related entity association as part of credential creation.
- [Delete a credential](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-sat-credentials/delete-a-credential.md): Delete a SAT credential so it can no longer be used for new extraction work.
- [List all credentials](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-sat-credentials/list-all-credentials.md): List SAT credentials available to your organization.
- [Retrieve a credential](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-sat-credentials/retrieve-a-credential.md): Retrieve a SAT credential by ID, including its credential type, RFC, and validation status.
- [Revalidate a credential](https://docs.syntage.com/api-reference/ds-mx-sat-credentials/revalidate-a-credential.md): Request validation for a SAT credential that is not already valid.
